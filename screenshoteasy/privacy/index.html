Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

ScreenshotEasy does not collect, transmit, or share any data.

  • No network access. The app never connects to the internet. It has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.
  • Screen content. The app reads your screen only when you start a capture. This requires the Screen Recording permission, which you grant in System Settings and can revoke at any time.
  • Where captures go. A capture is placed on the clipboard. It is written to a file only when you choose Save, in the folder you selected.
  • History. If “Keep recent screenshots” is on, your last 20 captures are stored inside the app’s own sandbox container on your Mac. Turning the setting off deletes them.
  • Text recognition. Text is recognized on your Mac with Apple’s Vision framework. Nothing is uploaded.

Deleting the app and its container removes everything it has stored.
