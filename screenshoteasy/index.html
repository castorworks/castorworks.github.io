Getting started

ScreenshotEasy lives in the menu bar and has no Dock icon. Click its icon in the menu bar, or press ⌃⌘A to capture a region and ⌃⇧3 to capture the full screen.

Common questions

Nothing happens when I capture, or the capture is blank

macOS needs your permission before an app can read the screen. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording, turn on ScreenshotEasy, then quit and reopen the app.

A shortcut doesn’t work

Another app may be using the same keys. Open Settings from the menu bar icon and record a different shortcut, or clear the one that conflicts.

Where do my screenshots go?

To the clipboard. A file is written only when you choose Save, by default into ~/Pictures/ScreenshotEasy. You can pick another folder and switch between PNG and JPEG in Settings.

How do I capture a menu or a tooltip that disappears?

Use Capture After Delay in the menu bar menu. You get 3 or 5 seconds to open the menu before the screen is captured.

How do I close or move a pinned screenshot?

Drag it to move it and scroll over it to zoom. Double-click it, or press Esc while the pointer is over it, to close it. Right-click it for opacity and actual size.

Which languages does text recognition support?

Chinese (Simplified and Traditional) and English. Recognition runs on your Mac.

How do I turn off the list of recent screenshots?

Turn off “Keep recent screenshots” in Settings. The captures already kept are deleted at the same time.

How do I change the app’s language?

Choose one under Language in Settings. It takes effect right away.
