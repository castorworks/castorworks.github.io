Zuneyra Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Zuneyra is operated by Chongqing Hyperits Network Technology Co., Ltd. It is a local music player for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. No account is required. The app has no advertising, behavioral analytics, or developer-operated data upload service.

Data stored on your device

Imported audio, metadata, artwork, lyrics, favorites, playlists, playback position, and preferences are stored locally. On Mac, security-scoped bookmarks for folders you authorize are stored on the device. We do not collect or receive this library data.

Files and system services

When you choose a system file provider, that provider may download or synchronize files under its own settings and policies. Music library backups and playlist exports contain the audio and collection information you choose to export; you choose where to save them. Lock Screen and system media controls display the current track. Apple services and system diagnostics are governed by Apple's settings and policies.

Removing data

Deleting songs from the library removes app-owned audio copies. Linked original files are retained unless you explicitly choose an operation that deletes the originals. Uninstalling the app may remove locally saved music and metadata; export a backup first if you wish to keep them.
