Zuneyra Support

Your personal offline music collection for iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

Import music

Use Import Music to select your own or licensed audio with the system file picker. On iPhone and iPad, Finder File Sharing is also available. On Mac, choose to copy audio into your library or link a music folder. Keep linked folders and external drives available while playing.

Browse and play

Browse Albums, Songs, Artists, Favorites, and Playlists. Open the full player from the bottom playback bar. Tap the artwork to switch to local lyrics. Use the queue to arrange upcoming tracks.

Backups and playlists

Open Manage Library to export a backup or transfer M3U8 playlists. To restore a backup, choose the .zuneyra-backup folder containing manifest.json and Media. For M3U8 import, choose the folder containing both the playlist and its audio files. Export a backup before removing the app or its data.

Contact

Email riclavaz@gmail.com with your device model, operating system version, and steps to reproduce the issue. Avoid sending private audio or personal information unless needed to explain your request.
